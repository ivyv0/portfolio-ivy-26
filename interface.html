Context

Interface in Motion was created for the course Interface & Beweging within the Minor Visual Interface Design. The course focused on how animation and motion graphics can be used in interactive applications to communicate information, improve interaction and make digital experiences feel more fluid and engaging.

Research

During the project, I explored animation principles, motion in interface design, dynamic icon systems and interactive animation. I also researched different tools and techniques to find a visual and technical direction that matched my concept.

Visual research was an important part of the process. I documented my creative development through references, experiments, storyboards and motion tests to build a clear direction for the final animated interface.

Design Challenge

The challenge was to create an interface where animation was not only decorative, but also functional. The movement needed to support the user experience by making transitions clearer, interactions smoother and the overall interface more expressive.

Process

I worked from concept to storyboard and then translated the idea into moving interface elements. Through practical exercises and feedback, I developed a motion style and tested how timing, rhythm, sound and movement could improve the interaction.

Outcome

The final result is an animated interface prototype that combines motion, interaction and visual style. The project helped me understand how animation can guide attention, communicate meaning and make an interface feel more intuitive and alive.
